The name is the year

Zero Nine. Because 2009 is when we started.

We began as a wholesaler supplying pharmacies and health shops around Nicosia with products that were hard to find on the island — gluten-free bread that a coeliac would actually eat, sweeteners a diabetic could bake with. The shop came later, because customers kept asking to buy direct.

Today we hold the Cyprus distribution for several of the brands on this site, which is why the prices here match the shelf and the stock is real.
